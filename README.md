# EXP.NO.1-EXPERIMENTAL-VERIFICATION-OF-VARIOUS-TYPES-OF-SAMPLING-TECHNIQUES
 

1.Experimental Verification Of Signal Sampling Using Various Types Such as 
    i) Natural Sampling
    ii) Flat Top Sampling

## AIM
 To perform experimental verification of various types of sampling such as natural sampling and flat top sampling.
## APPARATUS REQUIRED
Trainer Kit, DSO(10MHz) , Patch Cords and Power Supply (0-30V)   
## PROCEDURE
1.Connect the function generator to provide a continuous-time analog input signal (e.g., sine wave of 1kHz).

2.Connect the sampling circuit that supports switching between natural and flat-top sampling modes.

3.Use another function generator or a timing circuit to generate the sampling pulses (usually a square wave of higher frequency, e.g., 8kHz or more).

4.Connect the output of the sampling circuit to an oscilloscope to observe the sampled waveform.

5.Optionally, connect the sampled output to a low-pass filter to reconstruct the original signal for comparison.

6.Set the sampling mode to natural sampling in the circuit.

7.Apply the input sine wave (e.g., 1kHz) to the circuit.

8.Apply the pulse train (square wave with short duty cycle, e.g., 10%) as the sampling signal.

9.Observe the output on the oscilloscope:

10.You should see portions of the input signal passing through during each sampling pulse.

11.Note the sampled signal and compare it with the original waveform.

12.Pass the sampled signal through a low-pass filter and verify that the original signal is reconstructed.

13.Similarly for sample and hold and flat top sampling.

14. Plot the graphs respectively and verify the output.
